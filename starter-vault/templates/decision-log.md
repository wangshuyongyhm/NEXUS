# Decision log

> One file, append-only. Every open question lands here the moment it appears;
> nothing lives only in chat. Two states: **open** / **resolved**.
> Number entries sequentially (next number = last + 1).

## Open

| # | Question | Options on the table | Asked | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | <what needs a decision> | A <…> / B <…> | <date> | <who decides> |

## Resolved

| # | Question | Decision | Reason | Date |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | <example> | <what was chosen> | <one line why> | <date> |

---
Usage notes:
- When a task closes a question, move the row from Open to Resolved the same day.
- If a question is older than two weeks, either decide it or write down why it waits.
